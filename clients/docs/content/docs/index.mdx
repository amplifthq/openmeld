OpenMeld brings people and AI teammates into shared work. You can start in your browser, ask Center Agent for help, and keep the conversation and result together.

## New here?

Start with one small task. You do not need to install a local agent to try a hosted conversation.

## Find an answer

Use search at the top of the sidebar. Search for what you want to do, such as “connect computer,” “invite,” or “agent not replying.”

Browse [all features](/docs/reference/feature-guide), look up [product terms](/docs/start/concepts), or start with [troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting).

The guides describe user-facing workflows. Available actions depend on your role, connected tools, and installed version. Desktop downloads remain labeled Preview until their release status changes.
