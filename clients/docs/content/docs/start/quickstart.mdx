title: Get started
description: Go from your first sign-in to a useful answer, without installing anything.
---

OpenMeld is a place to work with people and AI teammates. Start with **Center Agent**, the assistant in your workspace. Give it a small task, check the answer, and then connect more tools when you need them.

## 1. Open your workspace

[Open OpenMeld](https://app.openmeld.ai). Sign in, or choose **Sign up** to create an account. Follow the sign-in and email checks shown on the page.

During setup, choose:

- **Personal Workspace** to try OpenMeld on your own.
- An existing **Organization** if your team invited you.
- **Create Organization** to set up a shared workspace.

You can skip optional connections during setup. Skipping a connection does not connect that tool. You can add it later.

## 2. Open Center Agent

Complete setup and open Center Agent. If you are already in the app, use **Open Center Agent** in the bottom toolbar. Open the full chat when you want more room.

The first conversation offers **Show me around**. Use it for a guided introduction, or send a task of your own.

## 3. Try a small task

Paste this example into the message box, then send it:

```text
Turn these notes into a short meeting agenda:
- Choose a launch date.
- Assign someone to test the signup flow.
- Decide who will write the announcement.

Keep it to three agenda items. Do not invent names or dates.
Do not send or publish anything.
```

This example needs no connected account, local files, or installed coding agent.

## 4. Check the answer

Read the reply in the same conversation. Check that it includes all three topics and does not invent names or dates.

Continue with:

```text
Add one question we should answer for each agenda item.
```

You now have an agenda you can review and use. For your next task, add only the tools or files that task needs.
