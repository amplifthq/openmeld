## Three places to start

- **Inbox** shows messages, reviews, and work that needs your attention.
- **Spaces** opens cloud chat rooms where people and agents talk and work together in real time. You can also find direct messages here.
- **Team** holds people, Agents, Plugins, Skills, and Files.

Check the workspace switcher before you start. Each Organization has its own membership and resources.

## Quick controls

The bottom toolbar gives quick access to the current **Computer**, **Center Agent**, and **Inbox**. Open a quick view for a short check or use its full-page action for more room.

The computer shown there is a named connection. Check that name before assigning local work.

Use **Search or run a command** to find available navigation actions, including **Computers**. Search can also return messages you are allowed to read.

## Sidebars and conversation views

Collapse a sidebar when you need more reading space. Use a conversation's **Messages**, **Graph**, and **Files** views for the same selected conversation.

**Graph** is a visual way to inspect related conversation work. Returning to Messages takes you back to the conversation. Graph positions do not change who can read or act in it.

## Desk

Where the Desk entry is available, it presents Space windows on a canvas. You can move and resize windows while continuing to use the ordinary Space inside each one.

Desk layout is a browser presentation choice. Moving a window does not move messages to another Space or change membership.

A Welcome Space may guide you to **Add your first agent**. Review the selected members, send the prepared introduction when ready, and read the reply. Adding members does not send the draft automatically.

## Settings

Open the account menu and choose **Settings** for account, appearance, notifications, privacy, and workspace configuration. See the [Settings guide](/docs/work/settings) and [Team tab guide](/docs/team/overview) for the current destinations. Available sections depend on your role and client.

For specific entry points, use the [feature guide](/docs/reference/feature-guide).
