OpenMeld lets people and agents work together without making every participant’s private information shared. Access controls determine which resources a request can use. Agent permissions control tool actions. Information-protection guidance helps agents decide what they may disclose to the current audience.

These protections work together. An instruction to keep something private is different from a file-access restriction enforced by the computer.

For example, a teammate can ask your Local Agent to review a project. That request does not authorize it to publish your private messages or credentials. Its tool access still depends on its permission mode and your computer’s file-access policy. Before sharing a result, the Agent must consider the destination and the information it contains.

## Find your controls

- **Settings → Security**: Organization information protection, available to owners and admins.
- **Settings → Privacy & Safety**: personal memory and computer file-access controls.
- **Agent settings**: the supported local tool’s permissions mode.
- **Space settings → Share & Access**: the Space’s audience and sharing controls.
- **Settings → Center Agent**: shared configuration, Audit Log, and pause or resume.

These guides explain product behavior. The [Privacy Policy](https://openmeld.ai/privacy) describes data processing and privacy terms.
