---
title: Find people and tools in Team
description: Use Team tabs to find teammates, agents, shared capabilities, files, and routines.
---

Open **Team** and check the workspace name. Its tabs describe resources in the current workspace; changing tabs does not switch Organizations.

| Tab | What to do here |
| --- | --- |
| People | Find teammates and open their profiles. Manage membership and roles in **Settings → Members**. |
| Agents | Find Agent Profiles, inspect availability, and open the intended teammate. See [Agents](/docs/work/agents). |
| Plugins | Find external tools and review personal or Organization connections. See [Plugins](/docs/team/plugins). |
| Skills | Find reusable procedures, install or create Skills when permitted, and inspect saved instructions. See [Skills](/docs/team/skills). |
| Files | Browse Team files, My files, and Trash, plus managed Team Knowledge. See [Files](/docs/work/files). |
| Routines | Find scheduled work and inspect its configuration and history. See [Routines](/docs/work/routines). |

Team is a directory, not a blanket access grant. A private Space, another member's private files, and personal external accounts retain their own access rules.

## Manage the Organization

Use [Settings](/docs/work/settings) for workspace identity, members, invitations, security, Center Agent configuration, and AI usage. Use [Workspaces and members](/docs/team/workspaces) to choose the correct workspace and manage team access.

## Find conversation work

[Tasks](/docs/work/tasks), Messages, Graph, and conversation Files live inside their conversation. Open the relevant Space or Center Agent chat to review them. A Task in a private conversation does not become Team-visible just because its assignee appears in Team.
