title: Workspaces and members
description: Keep personal work separate from shared team work.

## Choose a workspace

Use the workspace switcher to check where you are before adding files, connecting tools, or creating work.

A **Personal Workspace** is for you. An **Organization** is for shared work with members. Creating an Organization does not move your personal files, conversations, or connected tools into it.

## Create or join an Organization

Use the workspace switcher or account setup to create an Organization, join with an invitation, or select an existing membership.

Choose a recognizable name. If your team already has an Organization, ask for an invitation instead of creating a duplicate.

## Invite teammates

Owners and admins can manage invitations.

1. Open the Organization's member or invitation settings.
2. Enter the intended email addresses, or create a join link if that option is available.
3. Check the Organization and role.
4. Send the invitation and inspect its status.

An invitation sent is not a member joined. Check membership after acceptance.

Share join links only with their intended audience. Review or revoke unused invitations through their management controls.

## Roles

Owners and admins have management capabilities that ordinary members do not. Specific features can add narrower roles, such as [Skill Maintainer](/docs/team/skills).

If a control is missing, first check the active Organization and your role. Do not use another person's account to work around a restriction.

## Team versus Space access

Being in the same Organization does not grant access to every private Space. Add people to the appropriate conversation deliberately.

Before removing a member, review their ongoing work and shared assets. Removing access and deleting shared work are different actions.
