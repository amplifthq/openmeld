---
title: Find people in your team
description: Find a teammate without changing Organization membership.
---

Open **Team → People** in the intended workspace. Find the teammate and open their profile. Check that you are addressing the correct person before sharing work.

The People directory is different from membership administration. Use **Settings → Members** to manage roles and **Settings → Invitations** to invite or review joining members. See [Workspaces and members](/docs/team/workspaces).

Organization membership does not automatically grant access to every private Space. Add people to a conversation deliberately and check its audience. See [Space access and sharing](/docs/team/access).
