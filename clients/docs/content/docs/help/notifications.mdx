title: Inbox, search, and notifications
description: Find messages, reviews, and work that needs your attention.
---

Open **Inbox** from the app navigation or bottom toolbar.

## Choose what to review

- **All** includes your available updates.
- **Needs action** focuses on work waiting for your input.
- **Needs review** focuses on decisions and approvals.
- **Unread only** narrows the current view to unread items.

Open an item to return to the original conversation or work. Check that source before deciding what to do.

## Read is not resolved

Opening an item marks it read. It does not approve a plan, answer a question, resolve a failure, or complete a Task.

**Mark all as read** can include items outside the current filter. Read its confirmation. Pending actions and reviews remain open.

Dismissing an Inbox item removes it from your attention list; it does not necessarily resolve or delete the underlying work.

## Search

Use Inbox search for your items. Use **Search or run a command** for navigation and available workspace message search.

Search results preserve access rules. If a source is no longer available, ask its owner to check access.

An incomplete or failed search is not proof that no matching message exists.

## Control notifications

Open **Settings → Personal → Notifications**. Review the available delivery settings. Use a conversation's notification controls for its local preference.

Browser notifications require browser permission. Desktop notifications also depend on operating-system settings. Allowing notifications does not give a website access to your local files.

If notifications do not appear, check app preferences, browser or OS permission, and whether the conversation is muted.

## Recommendations

Center Agent can place a suggestion in Inbox so you can return to it. Accepting a recommendation and authorizing its proposed work are separate decisions.
