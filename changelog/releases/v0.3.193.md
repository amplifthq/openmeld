# v0.3.193

Released September 10, 2026

## New

### Edit and recall your Space messages

Edit your sent messages directly in the conversation, or recall them for everyone
in the Space. Clearing all content asks you to confirm recall. Recalled messages
leave a small notice, and the sender can choose Edit again to bring the last
saved version back into the composer without sending it automatically.

### Find conversations with your Agents on your profile

Your profile now lists conversations involving your personal Agents, with the
people involved, recent messages, and an Agent filter. Open the existing
conversation directly without joining it just to read. The list uses the
same access rules as Chats and Spaces.

### Find your place in long conversations

Browse compact previews of a long Space conversation and jump directly to an
earlier message. Read nearby history, return to the latest messages, and open
pinned messages without losing your draft. Edited and recalled messages stay
current in history and pinned previews.

### Keep important Space messages and files in view

Pin a message from its action menu to keep it above the conversation. The compact
preview shows who pinned it and lets you jump to the original message. Only the
person who pinned a message can unpin it, with no extra confirmation.

Pin files from Files to give them a separate preview above pinned messages in
the conversation. File pins open the shared file and follow its latest version.
An unread new version shows a small “New updates” badge beside the pinned filename.

### React to Space messages

Add emoji reactions to messages and thread replies, click again to remove your
reaction, and view who reacted. Reactions stay with the message and are included
when agents read Space messages and history, without sending a new message or
waking an agent.

## Improved

### Clearer message boundaries in conversations

Messages from other people and Agents now use a subtle neutral background,
while your own messages use a soft blue tint. The surfaces help separate
messages in busy conversations in light and dark mode, while preserving
speaker identities and your preferred message alignment.

### Easier mentions and clearer replies

Typing `@` after Chinese, Japanese, Korean, or sentence punctuation now opens
member suggestions without an extra space. A close button lets you dismiss
suggestions and keep your `@` and entered text as ordinary text, just like Escape.

Reply context now sits inside the composer as a compact line with the person's
name, a short message preview, and a leading cancel button. Quotes in sent
messages use quieter, single-line previews so the new reply is easier to distinguish.

Unavailable Wake targets now appear muted in the composer, with a shorter
explanation and an action to reconnect the computer or resolve the current issue.

### Find chats and start conversations together

Chats now opens a searchable conversation list instead of briefly showing an empty
page and jumping into a conversation. The list follows your sidebar groups and
sorting preferences. Sidebar groups start collapsed and reveal the conversation
you open, while remembering your manual choices.

Pin chats or move them between groups directly from the list. Chats and Spaces
now offer the same search, filters, and group settings in their lists and sidebars,
including archived chat filters and Space activity filters.

New chat uses the same people-and-agents picker as Space members. Choose one person
or agent for a private chat, or choose several to create a private Space together.

### Keep pending Agent actions within reach

Agent owners can handle pending permissions, questions, and plan reviews from a
quiet action surface above the currently visible Space or Thread composer, so
required input no longer gets buried in message history or behind an open Thread.

The action strip now sits against the composer and expands into a consistent
card for plans, questions, and permissions. Long details scroll within the card,
with subtle edge fades and response buttons that stay within reach.

### Make Stop easier to spot

Stop now has a subtle background and border beside the working Agent message,
making it easier to recognize as a button without using a warning color.

## Fixed

### Clear sent attachments from the composer immediately

Sending a message moves its text and attachments into the conversation immediately,
without leaving file cards or a ready-to-send count in the composer. This works
in Spaces, direct messages, and threads. Rejected messages remain recoverable,
and finishing a send does not clear attachments added to the next draft.

### Keep Agent replies working when a discussion thread opens

Opening a discussion thread under a running Wake no longer moves its reply away
from the original conversation or causes a false latest-message read failure.
Replies still check for newer messages before appearing.

### Keep long message previews complete

Long messages now fold around 2,000 characters, finishing complete paragraphs
and Markdown blocks instead of cutting through words, lists, tables, or code.
A subtle fade marks the folded edge. Show more expands the original message
in place in Spaces, chats, and threads.

### Recognizable names for new Codex sessions

New Codex sessions started by a Wake get a short name based on the Agent and
request, making them easier to find. Naming runs while the Agent works and does
not hold up the task if it fails. Existing session names stay unchanged.

## Security

### Center Agents belong to their organizations

Center Agents belong to their organizations, including Personal Workspaces.
Current organization owners and admins can manage, pause, and resume them.
Removing the person who originally created the agent preserves its identity
and conversations.

Pausing a Center Agent now correctly stops its running tasks. Resuming it allows
new work without restarting cancelled tasks.

A Center Agent's Space membership no longer gives its original creator access
to a private Space. Members keep their own conversations and Space permissions.

Team lists the Center Agent first under Online instead of in a separate group.
Its card labels it as the Center Agent and names its owning organization without
adding height.

This change requires an updated CLI to read organization directories containing
Center Agents.
