# v0.3.201

Released September 13, 2026

## Improved

### Align the Inbox status indicator with its icon

The sidebar Inbox now shows unread and attention indicators on the icon's
upper-right corner, matching Spaces instead of sitting on the button's edge.

### Clearer loading feedback on Desk

Desk now shows a centered loading indicator while preparing your Space.

## Fixed

### Keep Stop available while agents work

Thinking and reply preview updates no longer remove an available Stop button
from an active Wake. This applies to the shared conversation controls used in
direct messages, group conversations, and threads.

### Recover missing Center Agent replies

Center Agent retries eligible missing replies automatically without repeating
completed actions. If it still cannot answer, your message stays saved and the
failure is shown clearly. When the original conversation is available, Continue
conversation can prepare a reply from its saved results without running tools or
other actions again.

### Recover page loading without an error-page flash

When page files become unavailable after an update, OpenMeld shows a loading
state while refreshing automatically. If recovery fails, a clear refresh action
appears. Unsaved edits and messages still being sent keep their existing
navigation protections.
