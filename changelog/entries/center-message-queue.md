---
id: center-message-queue
title: Queue messages while Center is working
kind: new
areas: ["spaces", "center-agent"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Plain-text messages in a private Center Agent conversation wait in a visible
queue while Center responds. Reorder or remove pending messages, or choose
Steer to apply one to the exact response it was queued for.

Messages wait when delivery is uncertain or the previous request stops or
fails. Removing a queued message does not stop the current response.
