kind: improved
areas: ["center-agent", "computers"]

Center Agent can inspect your Computer's connection and available execution
host observations alongside local Agent diagnostics in your private conversation.
Task checks include waiting reasons, pending decisions, and stop eligibility,
helping distinguish a connection problem from work that needs your attention.
Existing maintenance actions keep their permissions and require evidence of
completion before a repair is reported.
