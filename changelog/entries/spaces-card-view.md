Spaces and Direct messages open in a card view inside your existing groups.
Switch to the table whenever you need a denser view. Both views keep your
sorting, filters, unread updates, and Space actions.

Cards show up to eight member slots and adapt to narrower widths. Unread counts
stay beside the Space name, and the latest message appears as a clean text
preview without Markdown formatting marks.

Your browser remembers the Space view you choose and each person's grouped or
expanded Agent view.

Use Show message previews in the sidebar settings to show or hide message
previews across the sidebar and cards. Previews are off by default, and your
browser remembers the choice across pages and reloads.
Rows without previews keep their titles and actions centered, and title hints
open beside the full row without covering its text.
