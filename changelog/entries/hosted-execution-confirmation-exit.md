id: hosted-execution-confirmation-exit
title: See when an Agent's execution needs confirmation
kind: fixed
areas: ["agents", "spaces"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

When OpenMeld cannot confirm a hosted Agent's execution, the Space now shows
**Result awaiting confirmation** and keeps Stop available to authorized users.
It explains that later work may be waiting without claiming the request failed
or encouraging you to resend it. Temporary query problems retry less often;
invalid or unsupported responses pause automatic queries.
