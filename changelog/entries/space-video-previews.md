title: Play shared videos in conversations and Files
kind: new
areas: ["spaces", "files", "web", "desktop"]

Play videos shared by people or Agents directly in a conversation or Files,
and expand the preview without losing your position. Videos load when you choose
to play, keeping idle conversations light. Local uploads retain their preview
through sending, and unsupported formats remain available to download.
