title: See the full date when you hover a message time
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "web"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Message times in Spaces and direct messages show only the hour and minute.
Hover a time to see the full date and time the message was sent, such as
"Oct 29, 2026, 2:48:17 PM".
