id: space-history-slider-stability
title: Keep Space history navigation steady
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "settings"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

The history slider now keeps messages in place while its directory loads and
stays steady when the Space header changes height. In Appearance, you can hide
the slider without turning off history navigation. Returning to a Space can
restore its history directory from this browser while it refreshes.
