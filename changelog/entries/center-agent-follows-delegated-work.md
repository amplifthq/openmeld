---
id: center-agent-follows-delegated-work
title: Center Agent follows through on delegated work
kind: improved
areas: ["center-agent", "spaces"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

When a delegated Agent's result needs review, another step, or a decision, Center
Agent keeps responsibility for that follow-up. It resumes after the Agent's
result, checks what happened, and either continues the work or says whose action
is needed next. The Agent does not need to mention Center Agent to hand the
result back.
