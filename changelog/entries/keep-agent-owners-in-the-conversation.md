title: Keep Agent owners in the conversation
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "direct-messages", "agents"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Adding a teammate's Agent to a Space now brings its owner in too. In a Direct
Message, the owner can review the conversation read-only, everyone can see that
they have access, and the owner can explicitly join when they want to
participate. If that Direct Message later becomes a Space, the owner becomes a
member and receives the same future updates as everyone else.
