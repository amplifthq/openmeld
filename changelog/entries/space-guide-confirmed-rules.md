title: Save confirmed Space rules without losing another owner's edits
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "web", "center-agent"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

When members agree on a lasting rule, the Center Agent replies with wording
a human Space owner can add to the Guide tab. If the guide changes while the owner
is editing, their draft stays open and they can review the latest guide before
saving. Later Space Wakes use the saved guide.
