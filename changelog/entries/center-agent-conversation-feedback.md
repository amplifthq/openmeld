title: Clearer progress in Agent conversations
kind: improved
areas: ["center-agent", "spaces", "settings"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Center Agent conversations show the Bot, progress, elapsed request time, and Stop
as soon as you send. New chats follow the latest message, preserve your avatar,
and avoid temporary delivery and membership states that interrupted the conversation.

Model-provided reasoning now appears in the existing work disclosure in hosted
Agent conversations. One-to-one direct messages omit redundant reply quotations,
and new arrivals do not introduce a New messages divider while you remain in the
conversation.

Appearance settings includes one switch for Bot avatar animations. Turn it off
to keep avatars still while Agents work and when you interact with them.
