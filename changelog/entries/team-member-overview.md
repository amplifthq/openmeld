title: A simpler Team overview
kind: improved
areas: ["web", "team"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Team opens a grouped member overview with the same organization as its sidebar.
Its header follows the other app pages, with member search, group filters,
counts, and an invitation action for members who can invite teammates.
Sidebar groups start collapsed and reveal the selected person's group, while
remembering manual expand and collapse choices.
Member rows focus on names, computer status, recent activity, and local agents.
The overview uses distinct cards with larger avatars to make members easier to scan.
You appear first in your group without being automatically starred. Saved stars
and custom groups remain available, and personal profiles no longer show the
Advanced details section.
Hidden sidebar group actions no longer reserve space beside unread counts.
