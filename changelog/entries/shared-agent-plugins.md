Plugins has its own place in the main navigation. Manage personal and shared
accounts there, with separate no-access, read-only, or read-and-write settings
for the Center Agent, Local Agents, and personal Remote Agents.

Local Agents can discover and invoke authorized plugin actions through the
OpenMeld CLI. Personal connections stay tied to their owner's requests;
shared connections follow organization membership and the configured access.
