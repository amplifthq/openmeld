Desktop settings now includes Open at login and Start in background. Choose
whether OpenMeld opens when you log in to your computer and whether its window
stays hidden at login. Opening the app yourself still shows the window.

The page also explains how closing or quitting affects desktop notifications
and the independent OpenMeld Service.
