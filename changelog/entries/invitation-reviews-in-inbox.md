id: invitation-reviews-in-inbox
title: Review invitations directly in Inbox
kind: improved
areas: ["inbox", "organizations"]
audiences: ["admins"]
---

Approve or decline invitation requests directly in Inbox under Needs review.
Approved invitations that still need follow-up appear under Needs action.
Settings → Invitations also lets admins approve or decline requests in a table,
with pending requests first and shared records of each decision, reviewer,
time, and invitation status. Direct invitations and approval requests now share
one recipient-grouped table, with clear actions in a separate column.
Inbox titles also show a compact status dot: red for pending work, yellow for
unread urgent updates, and blue for other unread items.
