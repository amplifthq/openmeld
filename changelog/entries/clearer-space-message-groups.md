title: Clearer Space message groups
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "direct-messages", "web"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Consecutive messages from the same person sit together, while different speakers
have more room between them. Space conversations use the full available width
by default, with message bubbles that fit their content. The first message in a
group shows a simple 24-hour time beside the name; later messages leave the time
to Message details. Direct messages keep their compact layout and show each
message's hour-and-minute time in its toolbar.

Actions float beside each message without moving the conversation on hover.
On narrow screens, the toolbar stays on the opposite side of your message and
keeps the remaining actions in More.
