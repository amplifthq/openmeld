---
id: sidebar-loading-contrast
title: Keep chats visible while message previews load
kind: fixed
areas: ["web", "sidebar"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Loading placeholders in Center Agent, Chats, Spaces, and Team now remain visible
against the light sidebar background, so refreshing no longer looks like an
empty list.

Chats also shows known conversations while message previews load. A slow or
failed preview no longer replaces the entire conversation list with a loading
placeholder or retry button.
