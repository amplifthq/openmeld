Desk opens a real Space you can work in immediately. If your workspace has no
Spaces, it creates a private Welcome Space. Messages, Files,
members, and sharing use the same interface as the full Space page. Scroll to
work, zoom out to see the canvas, or open the Space full screen. Your viewport
is remembered in this browser. Drag the Space window’s edges or corners to resize
it; its size is also remembered. Right-click the canvas to reset the view or
restore the default layout. Personal workspaces can also select their
Center Agent in the shared Add People or Agents dialog.

Adding people or agents from an empty Space opens the dialog directly without
also opening the Members panel, both on Desk and on the full Space page.
The picker groups agents beneath their owners and offers New Agent in the
same dialog.
Connecting a computer while creating an Agent stays in the same dialog, so you
can return to creating your Agent without leaving the Space.

Welcome Spaces start with you and a short guide to adding an agent through one
dashed action card. Center Agent is recommended and preselected the first time;
you choose whether to add it, create your own Agent, or add both. Adding members prepares an
editable introduction addressed to those members; you decide when to send it.
If you clear an unsent draft, the card lets you prepare it again. Once the
conversation starts, the card disappears instead of covering the date and messages.
Welcome progress and labels are remembered
in this browser, including when you open the Space full screen.

Hide a Space from Desk with its close button, then restore it through Add Space.
Add Space is available in the canvas context menu, the compact canvas toolbar,
and on an empty Desk. Search existing Spaces or open the standard Create Space
flow. Multiple Space windows retain their positions and sizes after refresh.
The toolbar expands on hover, keyboard focus, or tap. Tutorial controls are no
longer shown inside Spaces or on Desk.

Desk windows fit the available canvas with room around every edge and use their own conversation
layout with compact padding in narrow windows and a readable maximum width in wide windows. Resizing a window also adapts its Members
panel. Page layout preferences continue to apply when you open the full Space.

The first entrance reveals your greeting, then an arrow, then the Space, and
moves the view down to show the complete window. Returning restores your saved
view; reduced-motion preferences skip the entrance movement.
The greeting grows into view at a larger size, followed by a slower Space reveal.
Select the greeting to resize it or close it. This browser remembers dismissal.
