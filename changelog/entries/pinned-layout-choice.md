title: Choose a layout for pinned conversations
kind: improved
areas: ["web", "spaces"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Pinned conversations default to a grid with larger avatars and names underneath.
Use the Pinned layout menu to switch to a list. Your choice is saved in this
browser for the current profile and organization. Small gaps keep the grid's
hover highlight continuous; empty cells in the last row no longer highlight a
nearby conversation.
