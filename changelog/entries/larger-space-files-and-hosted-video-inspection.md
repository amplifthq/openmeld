title: Share larger files and ask hosted agents about videos
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "remote-agent", "center-agent"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Share files up to 1 GB in Spaces and direct messages. Center and Remote Agents
can download these files, attach them to replies, and send large Computer files
back through resumable uploads. For a video, ask an agent to inspect a specific
part: it samples frames and transcribes that part's audio, then tells you which
time range it covered. Longer videos can be inspected in additional parts.
