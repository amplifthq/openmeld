id: space-guide-save-and-leave
title: Save guide edits before leaving a Space
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "web", "desktop"]
audiences: ["members", "admins"]
---

When you leave a Space with unsaved guide edits, choose **Save and leave** to
save the draft and continue. If the save fails, you stay in the Space with
your edits so you can try again.
