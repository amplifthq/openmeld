title: Faster, clearer Spaces loading
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "web"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Spaces and Center Agent conversations appear together as soon as their list is ready. Message previews load with the list where available, and brief reads no longer flash placeholder rows or "Loading message" under conversations that already have a title. When a list or status cannot be read, the page shows that uncertainty instead of suggesting there are no conversations or no activity.
