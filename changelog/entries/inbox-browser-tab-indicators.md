title: See Inbox updates in your browser tab
kind: improved
areas: ["inbox", "web"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

The browser tab and Inbox entry show red for items that need action, amber for
unread urgent updates, and blue for other unread updates. The tab title includes
the relevant count, so you can see what needs attention while working elsewhere.
Reading a request does not clear its action indicator until it is handled or
dismissed from Inbox.
