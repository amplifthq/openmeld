---
id: local-agent-public-progress
title: Follow a Local Agent's progress while it works
kind: improved
areas: ["agents", "spaces"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Local Agents can share brief, deliberate progress updates in a Space while a
long-running Wake is still active. These updates remain separate from the
Agent's final reply, so collaborators can follow meaningful milestones without
mistaking progress for completion.
