title: Add Space members in a focused dialog
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "web"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Members can open the same Add People or Agents dialog from the Members header,
the one-member empty state, or Space setup guidance. The focused picker groups
each person's Agents beneath them, supports selecting either a person or an
Agent, and makes owner participation clear without adding nested cards to the
roster. The Organization's remote Center Agent appears first, clearly labeled
Remote.
