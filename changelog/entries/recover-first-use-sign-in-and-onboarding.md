title: Recover sign-in and first-use setup
kind: fixed
areas: ["authentication", "web", "desktop"]

---

Email verification requested during password sign-in now keeps the original
Desktop authorization or invitation destination. If your account profile cannot
load during first-use setup, the welcome page shows the error and lets you retry
before choosing a workspace or opening the tutorial.
