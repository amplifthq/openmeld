title: Keep sidebar lists readable while resizing
kind: fixed
areas: ["navigation", "spaces"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Contextual sidebar lists now collapse directly from their minimum readable
width. Dragging and keyboard resizing no longer leave a squeezed icon-only
state. Longer names stay on one line with an ellipsis, and edge hover still
reveals a temporary preview.
