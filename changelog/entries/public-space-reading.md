---
id: public-space-reading
title: Share a Space for reading without sign-in
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "web"]
audiences: ["users", "developers"]
evidence: ["pr:1103"]
---

Space owners can explicitly enable public link reading for retained messages,
Threads, files, retained image attachments, shared context, pins, and Tasks. Older message links open in
context, and automated readers can discover the same content through JSON links.

Reading and signing in do not join the Space. When guest access is also enabled,
external visitors can choose to join only that Space and return to the original
message or Thread. Closing link access blocks subsequent anonymous reads.
