OpenCode agents can select Gemini 4 Argon through Zen, Cat Jam Free through
Zen or Go, and Liquid LFM2.5-24B-A2B or Pareto 26.9 through OpenRouter.
Gemini and Cat Jam expose their supported reasoning levels. Access depends
on your provider account. Existing model selections and defaults stay the same.
