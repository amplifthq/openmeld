title: More room for your work
kind: improved
areas: ["web"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Spaces, direct messages, People, and Agents now keep their navigation lists in
the main sidebar, leaving more room for conversations and profile details.
Groups still collapse, and long lists share one scrolling area. Settings keeps
its separate navigation panel.
Pin controls appear without shifting working and unread indicators.

Organize Spaces into personal groups, sort each section, or drag Spaces into
the order you want. Your groups and ordering follow your account across devices.
Create a Space or group from the Spaces section's plus button.
Section actions stay quiet until you hover or focus the group. Drag a whole
Space row to reorder it, or sort pinned Spaces by priority or when you pinned
them. Hovering long lists no longer recalculates the whole page's styles.
Opening a Space preserves the sidebar's scroll position. Space names brighten
for unread messages, selection, and hover, rather than simply being unopened.
Priority sorting brings requests for input, unread conversations, and active
work forward in every section. Pin controls appear when you hover or focus a row.
Orbs waiting for your input now play their own animation, while respecting
your reduced-motion preference.

Find people by name or email, filter by Computer connection, star frequent
contacts, and organize them into personal groups. Drag people to reorder or move
them between groups, and open a private conversation from a row's Chat action.
Smaller avatars and consistent spacing keep the directory compact.

Agents now supports the same favorites, personal groups, dragging, and quick
Chat actions. Filter by owner to find one person's Agents. Compact status dots
replace repeated status text, and Checking uses a quiet animated indicator.

Messages now supports pinned conversations, personal groups, section sorting,
and drag ordering. Start a conversation with a person or Agent from the section's
plus button. Groups and ordering sync across devices without changing who can
access a conversation.

Local previews accept localhost aliases on the configured development Web port
when saving sidebar groups and order. Failed saves show readable guidance
instead of raw JSON.
