id: personal-agent-owner-inbox
title: Know when someone contacts your Agent
kind: improved
areas: ["inbox", "chats", "spaces"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

New messages from other people in a conversation with your personal Agent
appear as urgent Inbox updates, even when you have not joined. Chats and Spaces
show ordinary unread indicators, and reading the conversation clears the update.
Browser alerts for these updates are grouped by conversation and minute.
Organization-managed Agents do not send their administrators these alerts.
