---
id: working-folder-settings-repair
title: Choose and repair Agent working folders reliably
kind: fixed
areas: ["agents", "desktop"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Desktop no longer mistakes a normal sign-in refresh for an account change when
you choose a working folder. Agent settings show the saved folder, retain your
input while loading or switching sources, and explain folder errors clearly.
Failed managed-folder preparation preserves your previous choice. Recovery
distinguishes a saved folder from an Agent that is ready for new work.

The Desktop folder picker now stays connected when you take more than five
minutes to choose a folder, within the picker’s existing time limit.
