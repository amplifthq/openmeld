title: Resize, hide, and peek at the sidebar
kind: improved
areas: ["web", "navigation"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Drag the main sidebar edge to switch between a full sidebar, icons only, or a
fully hidden sidebar. Hover at the left edge while it is hidden to briefly
open it without moving your page. Your sidebar width and collapsed groups stay
saved, with the same workspace menu, navigation shortcuts, and inset layout.

The header adds back and forward controls and an Inbox shortcut. Open your
account menu from the avatar in the footer, with Settings and a theme toggle
beside it. Switch or add signed-in accounts from that menu; the organization
switcher now focuses on choosing and managing organizations.

Secondary sidebar selection now moves smoothly between destinations, including
Settings sections, while keeping the existing layout and respecting reduced motion.

Space and Center Agent header tabs keep their existing spacing and typography,
with a shared hover background and a sliding selection underline.
