---
id: agent-settings-unknown-display
title: Keep unread Agent settings visibly unavailable
kind: fixed
areas: ["agents", "desktop", "web"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

When saved Agent settings cannot be read, the model, permissions, and
collaboration mode cards show that their values are unavailable instead of
showing defaults. Retrying restores the saved settings and editing controls.
Confirmed settings that use defaults continue to display them normally.
