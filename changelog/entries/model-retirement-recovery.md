Model settings can show reviewed retirement notices and their official source.
A planned date is a reminder, not an automatic shutdown.

When a model is confirmed unavailable for your connection, OpenMeld preserves
your selection and explains how to change that Agent's model instead of silently
using another one. Failure guidance opens the affected Agent. A single Inbox
item is visible to the Agent's current owner and tracks a confirmed model issue.
It clears when the model is changed or the Agent's connection is removed,
without rewriting read history or restarting old tasks.

The first notices cover Codex's ChatGPT sign-in retirement announcements. They
do not disable API-key access or the same model through another controller.
