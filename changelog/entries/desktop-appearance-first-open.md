id: desktop-appearance-first-open
title: Keep conversation appearance when Desktop opens
kind: fixed
areas: ["desktop", "spaces"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Desktop keeps the selected conversation width and message alignment when it
opens. Conversations use the centered layout by default, and your messages
appear on the right in bubbles that fit their content, without selecting the
same settings again. Saved full-width and left-aligned preferences are preserved.

The macOS title bar matches the app's outer background in light and dark themes,
including when you switch themes.
