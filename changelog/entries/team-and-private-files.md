title: Keep team and private files in one familiar place
kind: new
areas: ["files", "team"]

---

Team Files gives members one place to browse files shared with the team and
files kept private to themselves. They can upload files, organize folders,
preview common document formats, inspect earlier versions, and copy a chosen
version into a Space. Agents use the same permission checks when working with
team files on a member's behalf.
