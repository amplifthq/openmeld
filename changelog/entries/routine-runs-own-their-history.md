title: Keep Routines running in busy Spaces
kind: fixed
areas: ["routines", "center-agent", "remote-agents"]
audiences: ["members", "admins"]
---

Scheduled Routines can start in a Space with a long conversation history. Each
Run now keeps its own working history while still posting the result to the
chosen Space, so older recurring Routines can continue on their next scheduled
occurrence without being blocked by unrelated messages in that Space.
