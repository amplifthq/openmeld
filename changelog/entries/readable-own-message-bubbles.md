Your messages now use a subtle background and leading-aligned text in every
OpenMeld conversation. Messages placed on the right stay in a comfortably
bounded bubble, while messages placed on the left use the full conversation
width. Agent and teammate messages remain unfilled until you hover over them,
and every message keeps the same rounded hover highlight for quick scanning.
