title: Consistent surfaces across setup and conversations
kind: improved
areas: ["web", "agents"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

The selected Local or Remote Agent option now has a raised surface, and the
Beta badge sits one level above its card. Configuration cards share the same
surface treatment and standard corner radius, with a matching hover highlight.
The layout and option details stay the same.

Create and Join Space cards and permission choices now share that surface
treatment. Message composers use a raised frame with softer corners across
Spaces and Center Agent conversations.

Computer setup, Plugins, and Channels use consistent card surfaces. Skills and
Routines share a dashed empty state, with skill creation and installation actions
inside the empty card. Light-mode message backgrounds and borders are clearer.
Floating date and jump controls share a translucent finish, and hovering a
message reveals its actions without adding a background highlight.
