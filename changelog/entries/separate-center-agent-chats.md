title: Work in separate Center Agent chats
kind: new
areas: ["center-agent", "web"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Use New chat to start a separate conversation with your Center Agent, then
switch between conversations from Chats. Each chat keeps its own history,
draft, and files view, so you can work on several requests at once without
combining their conversation context. The chats share your Center Agent's
cloud Computer.
