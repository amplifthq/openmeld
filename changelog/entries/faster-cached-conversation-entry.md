id: faster-cached-conversation-entry
title: Faster returns to long conversations
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "direct-messages", "web"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Spaces and direct messages open a smaller message window while keeping older
messages available as you scroll. Saved reading positions restore around the
message you left. Direct messages reuse available list previews to reduce
background requests, and unread indicators respond sooner when you reach the
latest messages.
