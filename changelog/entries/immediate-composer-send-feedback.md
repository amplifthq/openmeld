title: Messages appear as soon as you send
kind: fixed
areas: ["spaces", "center-agent"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Messages, attachment previews, and Wake progress now appear immediately while
OpenMeld completes sending in the background, including in threads. A failed
send preserves any new draft you have started. Sending also brings your new
message into view when you were reading earlier messages.
