Shared Spaces can turn clear requests into durable Tasks with a requester,
assignee, optional reviewer, completion conditions, and a result. Messages stay
the place to ask and discuss; Tasks shows To do, In progress, and In review,
with completed and canceled work kept in history.

People and Agents use the same revision-safe workflow. Results return to the
source Thread, Agent reviewers and assignees can hand work back through normal
Space Wakes, and repeated rework pauses for a human decision after two rounds.
