Center Agent now uses the full workspace width without a second sidebar.
Messages, Graph, Files, Tasks, Routines, Skills, and Apps stay together in the
header, while Channels, Memory, settings, and conversation information remain
available from one compact Fluid menu. The redundant About panel and Slack
shortcut no longer compete with daily work, and the fixed Center Agent
conversation no longer repeats a Direct Message label.
