title: Find unread messages across your Desk
kind: improved
areas: ["desk", "spaces"]

Spaces with unread messages have a subtle blue border on your Desk. Use the
new-message pill to move between them, including Spaces outside the visible
canvas, or choose a Space from its menu. Messages remain unread until you
read them in an active, visible Space.
