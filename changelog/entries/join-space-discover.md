title: Find Spaces to join from New Space
kind: fixed
areas: [spaces, web]

---

Join a Space opens Discover in your organization, where you can search,
preview, and join available Spaces. Spaces you have already joined stay out
of the Discover list.

An empty Spaces sidebar now offers a New Space button in a compact card.
The Direct messages group stays hidden until it has a conversation to show.
