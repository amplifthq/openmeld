---
id: stable-space-reading-header
title: Keep Space tabs steady while reading
kind: fixed
areas: ["spaces", "web"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Messages, Tasks, and Files switch directly into the compact Space header after
a meaningful scroll away from the latest messages. Small scrolls no longer
collapse the header, and switching views keeps the tabs in place without
replaying an entrance animation. Returning near the latest messages restores
the expanded header.
