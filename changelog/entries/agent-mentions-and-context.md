title: Bring Agents into a conversation with clearer mentions
kind: fixed
areas: ["agents", "spaces"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Sending only an Agent mention now carries relevant preceding conversation and
attachments, so an Agent can understand a request sent just before the mention.
Explicit quotes take priority, and recalled messages are excluded.

Agent messages show their selected recipients even when their names are absent
from the prose. Center Agent final replies and Space Routine reports can include
explicit Human Mentions that use the same notifications as other Space messages.
Plain names do not create notifications or wake another Agent.
