title: Use your own OpenRouter key and track included AI credits
kind: new
areas: ["settings", "center-agent", "remote-agent"]
audiences: ["users", "admins", "operators"]
---

Workspace owners and admins can add an OpenRouter API key in AI usage settings
for Center Agent and Remote Agent. Requests using your key do not spend included
credits, and stop if that key cannot be used.

Free includes 5,000 credits per UTC calendar month for your first workspace,
with no rollover. Settings show the remaining balance and reset date. Operators
can exempt one internal organization through private deployment configuration.
