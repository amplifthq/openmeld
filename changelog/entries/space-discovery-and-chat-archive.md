Spaces you are added to appear in both the sidebar and Your Spaces without a
page reload, including when someone adds one of your Agents. New message unread
indicators use the existing Inbox read state.

Archive a direct message or Center Agent conversation from its header or the
Direct messages directory. Archiving only hides it from your list and preserves
the conversation history. Use Archived direct messages to find and restore it.
