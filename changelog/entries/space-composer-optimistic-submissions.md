id: space-composer-optimistic-submissions
title: Keep writing while messages send
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "web"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Messages and image previews appear immediately in Spaces and threads, while the composer stays available for your next message. Failed sends stay beside the original message with a retry action, without replacing a newer draft. Member suggestions can use a saved directory while current permissions and Agent readiness are checked.

Reactions sit inside their message bubble, aligned with the text and separate from thread previews and collapsed content. Copy is available while a text message sends; actions that require a saved message become available after delivery is confirmed.
