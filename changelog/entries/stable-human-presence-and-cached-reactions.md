title: Steadier online status and faster message reactions
kind: fixed
areas: ["people", "spaces"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Briefly switching windows or moving between Spaces no longer immediately marks
you offline. Online and Offline describe people; the Team toolbar and section
filters separately offer Computer connected and Computer disconnected.

Confirmed message reactions are saved with cached conversations, so returning
to a Space restores the text and its existing reactions together while current
data refreshes in the background.
