Local Agents gain Grok 4.7 in Cursor, Grok Build, and OpenCode Go; Grok 4.7
Fast in Grok Build; and MiMo V2.6 Flash Free, Flash, and Pro in OpenCode.
OpenCode's default moves to MiMo V2.6 Flash Free because the previous free
model is no longer available.

Choose GPT-6 Astra, GPT-5.6 Sol, Claude Fable 5.1, Claude Fable 5, or Grok 4.7
for your Center Agent and personal Remote Agents.

Both now use matching provider and model selectors. Browse a provider, choose
a model, and save your choice. Existing hosted selections and the OpenMeld default
stay the same.
