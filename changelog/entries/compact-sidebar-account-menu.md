title: A simpler, customizable sidebar
kind: improved
areas: ["web", "settings"]

The sidebar keeps its main navigation and a smaller account avatar. Open the
account menu for Search, Settings, and a compact Light, Dark, or System theme
selector. Search shows the Command K shortcut with keycaps that follow your
corner style. The same theme selector is
available in Settings > Appearance, and changing themes keeps the menu open.

Computer, Center Agent, and Inbox toolbar shortcuts now default to off. Enable
them in Settings > Appearance when you need them. Saved choices stay unchanged.

The Spaces and Team sidebar search fields stay aligned when switching sections,
without an entrance animation.

Hover over Team to jump to Agents, Plugins, Skills, Files, or Routines.
Hover over Spaces for Direct messages and Discover. Pin Direct messages to add
a compact DMs shortcut to your sidebar.
Pin the ones you use often to the sidebar, or unpin them from the same menu.
Your pins are saved in this browser. Sidebar icons now show their names as soon
as you hover over them.
