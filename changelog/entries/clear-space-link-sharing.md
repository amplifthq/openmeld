Share or invite now focuses on choosing how a Space link works, understanding
what recipients can do, copying it, and inviting people from the same flow.
Space settings separate Members from Share & Access, so member roles and
invitations stay visible without mixing them into link and guest policies. Both
sharing surfaces preserve one draft and save the policy in one explicit action.

Existing public and password-protected Spaces keep their current behavior.
