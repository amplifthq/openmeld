id: fluid-agent-avatars
title: Recognize Remote Agents by their fluid avatars
kind: improved
areas: ["agents", "center-agent", "chats"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Remote Agents now use soft-colored fluid avatars throughout creation, profiles,
and chats. Personal Remote Agents have rounded-square avatars; Center Agents
have circular avatars. Colors follow the Agent's name, including the creation
preview. The creation preview plays automatically. Elsewhere, avatars stay
still until hovered or working, and pause without jumping back to the start.
Animations respect reduced-motion preferences.
