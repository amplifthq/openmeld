id: space-composer-access-recovery
title: Keep message tools available while Space access is checked
kind: fixed
areas: ["spaces"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

The + menu and @ picker stay available while sending is unavailable, and the @ button opens the picker after existing text. The Composer keeps the reason visible and puts Join beside the draft when membership is required. You can restart a stalled access check without refreshing the page or losing your draft. Sending and file uploads still require confirmed access.
