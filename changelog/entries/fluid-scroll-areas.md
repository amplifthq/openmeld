title: Quieter, more consistent scrolling
kind: improved
areas: ["web", "desktop"]

Scrollbars now stay out of the way until you hover or scroll. Shared scroll
areas use a subtle edge fade to show where more content is available, including
settings, sidebars, conversations, and detail panels. Touch scrolling keeps its
native behavior.
