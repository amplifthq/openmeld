id: personal-remote-agent-routines
title: Schedule work with your Remote Agent
kind: new
areas: ["agents"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Ask your personal Remote Agent to run a task once or on a recurring schedule
and return the result to your chat or a Space. You can also ask it to list,
change, pause, resume, or delete its Routines. Scheduled work uses that Agent's
identity and checks access again before running.
