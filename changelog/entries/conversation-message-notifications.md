title: Choose which conversation messages notify you
kind: new
areas: ["inbox", "spaces", "settings"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Set an account default for message notifications, then use the bell in any
Space or Direct Message to follow that default, receive all new messages, keep
mentions and replies, or mute message notifications. Conversation choices stay
personal and do not change unread state or urgent email alerts.

Mentions and replies also notifies you when another person replies to a thread
you started. Self replies stay quiet, and muting the conversation still applies.

Notifications settings also includes Show an example to help check browser and
system notification settings. OpenMeld must remain open for browser delivery.
