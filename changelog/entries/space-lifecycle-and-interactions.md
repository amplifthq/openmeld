id: space-lifecycle-and-interactions
title: Manage Spaces and Direct Messages with consistent controls
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "direct-messages", "inbox"]
audiences: ["members", "admins"]
---

Newly joined Spaces appear immediately with a New indicator until opened, even
when they have no messages. Message badges count actual unread messages.
Pinning and dragging respond immediately. Discover shows member
avatars and a read-only preview; joining opens the full Space.

Adding a third participant turns a Direct Message into a Space permanently.
Personal Spaces settings let you choose whether messages automatically Wake the
Agent when you are the only Human with one Agent.
Conversation layout, message placement, and the history slider are now under
Personal Spaces settings, with visual choices. Message previews share the same
setting as the conversation sidebar.
You can also choose Enter or Cmd/Ctrl+Enter to send messages, including in
Direct Messages and threads.

When an Owner deletes a Space, its members can still read and search its history.
The Space shows a small Deleted label and accepts no new messages or Agent work.
Members can remove it from their own lists. Archived Direct Messages also offer
Delete for me, which clears the person's history without affecting other people.
