Plugins adds Google Sheets, Docs, Slides, Forms, Analytics, Search Console,
Meet, and Ads, plus Vercel, HubSpot, Airtable, Supabase, Outlook, Resend,
Meta, Mailchimp, and Canva. Sign-in availability depends on the OAuth setup
for each environment. Providers that support credentials can also use your
own token, API key, or service account.

Gmail, Outlook mail, Google Calendar, and Google Meet now connect under
Personal. You choose which Agents can use your account. Older organization
connections for these services are disabled and remain available to disconnect.
