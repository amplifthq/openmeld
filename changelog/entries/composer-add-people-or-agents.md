kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "composer"]

The composer’s Add menu now includes People or agents in eligible Spaces.
This entry and the empty Space shortcut open the member picker without opening
the Members panel. Message composers also have a cleaner layout without the
divider above their action buttons.
