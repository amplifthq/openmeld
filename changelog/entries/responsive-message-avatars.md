title: Clearer avatars in wider conversations
kind: improved
areas: ["web", "spaces", "chats"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Message avatars are slightly larger when your conversation has more room.
They stay compact on phones and in narrow panels, and grow in two steps as
the message area widens. People and Agents use the same sizes, with message
spacing adapting alongside them.
