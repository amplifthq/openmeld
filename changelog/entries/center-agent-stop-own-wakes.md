title: Let the Center Agent stop Wakes it started
kind: fixed
areas: [agents, spaces]
audiences: [users]
---

The Center Agent can request Stop for an exact Wake it started, or act for a
verified requester who could stop that Wake in Web. Wake progress shows when
Stop is available, and the result distinguishes a Stop request from a confirmed
stopped outcome.
