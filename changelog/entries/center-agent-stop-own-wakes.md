title: Let the Center Agent stop Wakes it started
kind: fixed
areas: [agents, spaces]
audiences: [users]
---

The Center Agent can request Stop for an exact Wake it started when its recorded
requester matches the current verified member. It can also act for a verified
requester who could stop the Wake in Web. Wake progress shows when Stop is
available, and the result distinguishes a Stop request from a confirmed stop.
