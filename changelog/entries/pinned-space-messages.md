---
id: pinned-space-messages
title: Keep important Space messages and files in view
kind: new
areas: ["spaces", "web"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Pin a message from its action menu to keep it above the conversation. The compact
preview shows who pinned it and lets you jump to the original message. Only the
person who pinned a message can unpin it, with no extra confirmation.

Pin files from Files to give them a separate preview above pinned messages in
the conversation. File pins open the shared file and follow its latest version.
An unread new version shows a small “New updates” badge beside the pinned filename.
