id: service-incident-diagnostics
title: Clearer evidence for local Service failures
kind: improved
areas: ["service", "diagnostics"]
audiences: ["operators"]
---

Setup failure diagnostics distinguish recent Service logs from old or undated
errors, include macOS product and build versions, and preserve structured
filesystem error and Service update operation details. This helps support
investigate permission incidents without uploading full system logs or treating
every permission error as an Apple App Management block.
