title: Connect from your Space and update Tools in Desktop
kind: fixed
areas: ["desktop", "computers"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Desktop can connect or reconnect your Mac from a Space, keep the original request,
and return to that Space after completion. OpenMeld Tools updates also run in
Desktop and verify the Service update before continuing. A mismatched account,
profile, Space or computer cannot complete the request.
