title: Less work when opening conversations
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "direct-messages", "center-agent"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Recent messages can load while Agent availability is still being checked.
Images farther up the conversation wait to download until you scroll near them,
and messages without reactions avoid unnecessary layout work.

Thread previews stay on one line. Select a reply to open its Thread, scroll to
that message, and highlight it. New Threads open immediately while they load,
and Center Agent chat avatars use the same lightweight directory as other chats.
