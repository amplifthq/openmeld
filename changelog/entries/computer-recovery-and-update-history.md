title: Clearer Computer recovery and update history
kind: improved
areas: ["desktop", "web", "service"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Computer owners using Desktop can continue an update or reconnect in their
browser, with instructions naming the computer that needs attention. Computer
cards distinguish the last failed update from the current connection and keep
the original update details available. A restored connection does not hide a
required update or imply that the requested version was installed.
