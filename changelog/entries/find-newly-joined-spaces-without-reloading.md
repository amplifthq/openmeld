title: Find newly joined Spaces without reloading
kind: fixed
areas: ["spaces", "web"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

When someone adds you to a Space, the Web sidebar now discovers it while the
page stays open and refreshes immediately when you return to the window. A new
Space stays at the top of its existing Pinned or Spaces group with a clear,
accessible **New Space** treatment until you open it, while unread messages keep
their amber indicator and normal unread meaning.
