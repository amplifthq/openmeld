title: Find newly joined Spaces without reloading
kind: fixed
areas: ["spaces", "web"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

The first time someone adds you to a Space, the Web sidebar now discovers it
while the page stays open and refreshes immediately when you return to the
window. A Space you have not opened stays at the top of its existing Pinned or
Spaces group with a clear, accessible **New Space** treatment until you open it,
while unread messages keep their amber indicator and normal unread meaning.
