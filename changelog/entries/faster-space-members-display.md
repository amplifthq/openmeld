kind: improved
areas: ["spaces"]

Previously loaded member names, avatars, and counts appear while a Space refreshes.
Member actions and availability still wait for current access and status checks.
Checking and working details appear in the status dot's tooltip without repeating
below the agent's name; recovery guidance stays visible when action is needed.
