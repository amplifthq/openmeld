---
id: protect-private-agent-context
title: Protect private Agent context in shared replies
kind: security
areas: ["agents", "privacy", "spaces"]
audiences: ["members", "admins"]
---

Agents now keep private conversations, messages, memory, drafts, and assessments
out of shared replies unless the owner clearly identifies what to share and who
should receive it. A role, Organization membership, or broad instruction to
share everything no longer counts as permission to disclose private context.
