kind: improved
areas: ["web", "team"]

Center Agent appears in its own group at the top of Team. Your profile starts
in Starred, and you can unstar it at any time. Other people appear in your custom
groups or Computer online and Computer offline. These labels describe computers,
not whether someone is using the website. People without a computer show No
computer, and connection filters remain available.

Home chat rows now offer a quick Pin or Unpin action beside the menu on hover
or keyboard focus. The message preview stays in place as the actions appear.
