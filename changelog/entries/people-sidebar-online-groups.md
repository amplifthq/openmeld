title: Simpler Team sidebar groups
kind: improved
areas: ["web", "team"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Your profile stays at the top of Team. Other people appear in Starred, your
custom groups, or the default Online and Offline groups. Hover over a default
group label to see that it describes computer connection. People without a
computer show No computer, and connection filters remain available.

Home chat rows now offer a quick Pin or Unpin action beside the menu on hover
or keyboard focus. The message preview stays in place as the actions appear.
