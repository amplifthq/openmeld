id: keep-space-sending-out-of-access-checks
title: Keep Space sending uninterrupted while a conversation opens
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "web"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Returning to a saved Space no longer interrupts the composer with an access
check or a separate retry button. You can keep writing and use the normal Send
button while OpenMeld safely confirms access in the background; if the message
cannot be sent, your draft stays in place so you can try again.
