areas: ["web", "spaces", "chats"]

Pinned conversations use larger avatars in a grid that adjusts to the sidebar
width. Two rows stay visible by default, with a control to show more and an
update indicator for hidden conversations. The shared Fluid hover highlight,
conversation menus, unread links, and drag ordering remain available.
