title: Browse attachments without stretching the conversation
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "center-agent", "web", "desktop"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Images, videos, and files stay in one compact, horizontally scrollable row in
conversations, Threads, and the composer. Image thumbnails fill their frames
without stretching; opening an image still shows the complete original.

Sent attachments sit above the text bubble while remaining part of the same
message. File names and type icons are easier to distinguish, corners follow
your appearance setting, and attachment errors remain actionable without
stretching the row.
