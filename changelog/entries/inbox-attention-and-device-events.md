Inbox separates mentions, actions and reviews, with independent unread and Agent
filters. Conversation groups keep routine activity compact while important open
requests remain easy to find. Blue and yellow indicators distinguish unread
updates from priority attention without turning every approval into an alarm.

The first outside contact with your personal Agent gets one awareness reminder;
routine follow-ups do not keep interrupting you. Unexpected Computer connection
loss and native Desktop updates use the same Inbox, with device-specific recovery
or update guidance and no duplicate copies when you switch organizations.
