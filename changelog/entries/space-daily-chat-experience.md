title: Clearer Space conversations and relevant notifications
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "inbox", "web"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Thread replies notify participants instead of everyone in the Space. Reactions
to your messages now appear as ordinary unread Inbox updates, including replies
inside threads.

One unread shortcut takes you to the first unread update. Thread updates
highlight their main message and show a blue **Open thread** entry with an
unread count; the thread opens only when you choose it. Inbox links still open
the exact reply, and historical failure links focus the retained request.
Reading the latest messages also acknowledges
newer updates attached to those messages. Sent messages update the sidebar
preview immediately while the shared index catches up.

Returning to a Space restores saved messages with their reactions and Thread
previews together. Recently opened threads keep their replies visible while
refreshing, and first-time opens use a quiet conversation area while replies
arrive. Headers and message composers appear while messages load, so you can
start a draft on a slow connection and keep writing when the conversation
arrives. Large threads also require less reply-body processing to show previews.

When an Agent cannot respond, choose **Mention only instead** to keep chatting.
Each Agent in the composer can independently use **Wake** or **Mention only**.
