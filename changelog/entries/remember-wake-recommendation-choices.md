title: Remember dismissed Wake suggestions
kind: fixed
areas: ["spaces"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Dismissed Wake suggestions stay dismissed when you leave a Space and return or
refresh the same browser tab. A new suggestion appears after a new Wake receives
a reply. Ignored suggestions move to the message you sent instead of repeating
in the Composer. Your unsent text, mentions, images, and files stay in your draft.
Close a Composer suggestion with its hover control or Backspace at the start of
your message, without accepting the suggestion first.
