title: Clearer navigation and Team actions
kind: improved
areas: ["web"]
audiences: ["users"]

Inbox now sits directly above Spaces in the main navigation, with its unread
and attention indicators. Global search moves above Settings at the bottom
of the rail. The Team sidebar has an Add menu for inviting members or creating
an Agent, alongside its existing people search and filters.

Opening the App now lands in your workspace's Spaces. Desk is temporarily
hidden from navigation and remains available through its direct link.
