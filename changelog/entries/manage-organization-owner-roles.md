---
id: manage-organization-owner-roles
title: Change another Organization Owner's role
kind: improved
areas: ["organizations", "settings", "web"]
audiences: ["admins"]
---

Organization Owners can change another Owner to Admin or Member from Settings
after confirming the change. The person remains in the organization, and at
least one Owner stays in control, including when role changes happen at the
same time. Admins and Members cannot change Owner roles.
