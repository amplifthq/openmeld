title: Reuse confirmed member identities in a Space
kind: fixed
areas: ["center-agent", "spaces"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

The Center Agent uses stable member identities and checks earlier Space
confirmations before asking again who a teammate or connected-app account
belongs to. Confirmed account mappings can be reused across threads even when
display names or emails differ between services. Conflicting evidence still
requires clarification, and existing access and memory policies remain in
effect.
