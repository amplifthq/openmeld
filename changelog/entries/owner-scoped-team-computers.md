Team Computer links now identify both the owner and device, so a device used
under multiple accounts cannot open another account's old record. Ambiguous
older links ask you to choose the Computer from its owner's Profile. Outdated
local-agent checks now say **Status out of date**, without implying that the
Agent version is outdated or that the owner must repair the Computer.
