id: computer-disconnect-inbox-recovery
title: Clear stale disconnect attention when a Computer reconnects
kind: fixed
areas: ["inbox", "computers"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

When a Computer reconnects, its disconnect incident automatically leaves Inbox
priority attention and Needs action. It no longer keeps a yellow warning or asks
you to act just because local work is not ready yet. The incident remains in
history, while unread state, other Computer alerts and separate Agent or task
failures are preserved.
