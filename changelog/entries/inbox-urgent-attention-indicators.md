title: See urgent updates and outstanding actions at a glance
kind: fixed
areas: ["inbox"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Inbox now includes unread urgent updates in its sidebar badge, alongside
outstanding actions, counting each item once. Red indicates an action waiting
for you; amber indicates unread urgent updates. The bottom Inbox shortcut uses
the same color without repeating the number, and its preview brings urgent
updates forward. Marking an action read does not remove it from the count.
