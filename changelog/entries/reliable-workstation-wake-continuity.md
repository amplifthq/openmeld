Workstation Agents can keep working through a temporary OpenMeld Service
connection replacement and through Codex reply repair without a false provider
or network failure. Deleting an Agent now durably retries active-Wake stopping,
local connection cleanup, and Space removal until all of them are confirmed;
the upgrade also repairs an old connection left behind by an already deleted
Agent, and its stopped notice explains that the Agent Profile was deleted.
Hovering or focusing a Space's Working orb identifies the Agent whenever frozen
Space identity is available, without adding another label to the compact
sidebar. Wake trace also keeps active execution ahead of generic final message
projection diagnostics, so its current state and next action stay truthful.
