title: Browse grouped Inbox updates with less clutter
kind: improved
areas: ["inbox", "web"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Long groups of Inbox updates scroll within a bounded area. Expand or collapse
all groups, and keep your choices when you return in the same browser.

Removing an item or group now asks for confirmation and explains that original
messages and documents remain available. Group actions also have clearer buttons.
