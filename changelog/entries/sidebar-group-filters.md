kind: improved
areas: ["web"]

Spaces now has the same hover search and filter actions as People and Agents.
Select multiple sidebar groups, including Pinned or Starred and custom groups,
and combine them with search and existing filters. Remove individual filter
chips to broaden the list. People also supports filtering to members active in
the last five minutes, separately from Computer connection status.
