title: Receive notifications while Desktop runs in the background
kind: fixed
areas: ["desktop", "inbox"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Desktop can deliver macOS notifications for directly addressed messages,
actions and urgent Inbox items independently of browser notifications. Personal
Notifications includes a Desktop switch, sound control and test notification.
Alerts continue with the window hidden while the app is running. Clicking an
alert restores Desktop and opens its source after checking access.
