kind: fixed
areas: ["web", "sidebar", "observability"]

Sidebar action buttons no longer shift the details and app icons below a person's name. Spaces receive message previews with their list data instead of polling each conversation separately. Slow navigation and interaction reports help operators investigate recurring performance problems.
