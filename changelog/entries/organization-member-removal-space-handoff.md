id: organization-member-removal-space-handoff
title: Resolve Space ownership when removing a teammate
kind: fixed
areas: ["organizations", "spaces", "web"]
audiences: ["admins"]
---

Organization admins can review and resolve a departing teammate's Space ownership
without asking them to return or joining their private Spaces. Eligible Spaces
used only by that person, including Welcome Spaces, close to retained read-only
history. Shared Spaces keep an existing owner or receive an explicitly selected
successor, with a clear notice when that person gains access to private history.

The removal dialog explains which Spaces will change before confirmation. If
ownership changes succeed but member removal does not, it reports the incomplete
removal and lets the admin review the remaining work and retry.
