title: Give clear answers about recent Center Agent requests
kind: improved
areas: ["center-agent", "channels", "slack"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

When you ask why a recent Center Agent request succeeded, failed, or produced
no reply, the Center Agent can now check the recorded execution and publication
facts for your recent requests in that conversation. It reports exact status,
failure details, and message IDs when available instead of guessing from reply
wording.
