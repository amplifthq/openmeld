Sidebar highlights follow reordered rows correctly. Space avatars, timestamps,
and hover actions have clearer spacing, and loading placeholders resemble the
rows they replace. People groups wait for their connection information instead
of briefly showing an incomplete arrangement.

Each page remembers its own sidebar width. Saving Agent settings also refreshes
the configuration and status shown elsewhere in the workspace.
