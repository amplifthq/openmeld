id: team-member-detail-controls
title: Return to Team and manage members from their profile
kind: improved
areas: ["team", "desktop"]
audiences: ["members", "admins"]
---

Member profiles include a direct return to Team and preserve the desktop navigation history. Owners and admins can review the impact of removing an eligible member and confirm the removal from the profile.

Organizations can optionally set a default ownership recipient for Space transfers that do not grant new access. The reviewer still confirms the named recipient.
