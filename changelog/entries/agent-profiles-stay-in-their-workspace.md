title: Keep Agent Profiles in their workspace
kind: improved
areas: ["agents", "spaces", "organizations", "cli"]
audiences: ["members", "admins"]
---

Each workstation Agent Profile now stays in the Personal Workspace or
Organization where it was created. OpenMeld no longer offers cross-workspace
Wake access, and a Space cannot Wake an Agent Profile from another workspace.
