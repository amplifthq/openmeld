title: Open browser alerts where attention is needed
kind: improved
areas: ["inbox", "notifications"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Browser notifications now open the exact Space message, Agent interaction,
Center Agent Task, document, setting, or connected-channel message that needs
attention instead of stopping at Inbox first. Members who have not decided
about browser notifications also see a one-time explanation before OpenMeld
asks the browser for permission.
