Send your first message, then try connecting a local agent while your Center
Agent replies. Prepared greetings and highlighted controls show what to do next.
Replies stay in the conversation, and the tutorial waits for you to continue.

After connecting your computer, Agent creation stays on that connection while
local agents appear. Connection commands use a single scrollable line, and
Terminal shows the full address to return to OpenMeld.
