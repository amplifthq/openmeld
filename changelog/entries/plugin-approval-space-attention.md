---
id: plugin-approval-space-attention
title: Keep pending approvals visible across Inbox and Spaces
kind: fixed
areas: ["inbox", "spaces", "plugins"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Pending Plugin approvals now give their source Space a yellow attention
indicator for the person who must respond. Inbox, the Spaces navigation,
the sidebar, and Space cards and tables consistently show pending human requests.
Reading a request keeps its yellow indicator. Approving or declining it clears
its pending attention while preserving any other unread messages.
