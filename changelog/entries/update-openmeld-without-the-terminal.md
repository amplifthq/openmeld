title: Update OpenMeld without the Terminal
kind: improved
areas: ["computers", "center-agent", "openmeld-service"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

New Computers keep OpenMeld Tools up to date by default. Members can also start
an update, change automatic updates, or switch a Codex installation from the
Computer page or Center Agent without copying a command.
