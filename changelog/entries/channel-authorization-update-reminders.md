id: channel-authorization-update-reminders
title: Permission update reminders for connected channels
kind: new
areas: ["channels", "inbox"]
audiences: ["admins"]
---

Organization owners and admins receive a Needs action item when Slack, Feishu or
Lark needs updated permissions. Eligible email reminders follow existing
notification preferences.

Open the reminder to update the existing connection. Once one owner or admin
completes authorization and the connection verifies its permissions, the request
is cleared and marked read for everyone. Pending reminder emails are cancelled.
