title: Center Agent chats share one sidebar entry
kind: improved
areas: ["center-agent", "spaces", "web"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Your private Center Agent chats now appear in the Spaces list as one entry,
such as **Felix Johnson’s Center Agent**, instead of one row per chat. The entry opens
the chat with your newest unread message, or your latest chat, shows unread and working activity from all of them, and
keeps its pin or group. Switch chats or start a new one from the chat title,
which now shows the entry name in front of the chat's own title. Searching lists
each matching chat on its own row. Changing your display name renames the entry,
your profile and your member listings right away.
