title: Center Agent chats share one sidebar entry
kind: improved
areas: ["center-agent", "spaces", "web"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Your private Center Agent chats now appear in the Spaces list as one entry,
such as **Felix’s Center Agent**, instead of one row per chat. The entry opens
your latest chat, shows unread and working activity from all of them, and
keeps its pin or group. Switch chats or start a new one from the chat title,
which now shows the entry name in front of the chat's own title. Search still
finds each chat by its title.
