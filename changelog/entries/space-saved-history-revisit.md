title: Pick up where you left off in Spaces
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "web"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Returning Spaces can show saved messages while access is checked. Loaded history
and your reading position can survive refreshes, new tabs, and browser restarts
within the browser's storage limits. Sending and other actions still wait for
current access confirmation.

Smaller blue new-message controls are easier to find without interrupting your
reading. The control for messages above now sits just below the date pill.

Messages sent through the CLI using your own profile now appear as unread until
you read them. Messages you send in Web do not create a reminder for yourself.
