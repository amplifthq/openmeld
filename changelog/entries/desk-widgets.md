Add Spaces to your Desk, then move, resize, or hide windows to organize your work.
Add editable text from the canvas context menu, and undo layout changes.
Your arrangement is remembered in this browser.

Window titles sit above their cards: drag to move, click to rename. Text labels
fit their contents.

New users still start with one Welcome Space and choose what to add next.
