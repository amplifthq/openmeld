Add Spaces, chats, Inbox, work updates, People & Agents, and computer status to
your Desk from a categorized picker. Rename, move, resize, or hide windows to
organize your work. Add editable text from the canvas context menu, and undo
layout changes. Your arrangement is remembered in this browser.

Window titles sit above their cards: drag to move, click to rename. Text labels
fit their contents. Desk Inbox includes recent read updates, while Active agents
identifies ongoing work in your Spaces and chats.

Choose one or more people and agents to add individual Profile cards with a
shortcut to chat. The picker marks cards already on your Desk, and makes them
available again when you close them.

New Profile cards fit their contents and adapt their layout as you resize them.
Idle agents omit the work summary, and update and computer windows start at more
compact sizes.

New users still start with one Welcome Space and choose what to add next.
