title: Start Agent work without an extra maintenance check
kind: improved
areas: ["agents", "service"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Waking an Agent no longer waits for a full Service installation and process
inspection just to add update advice to its instructions. Update and repair
guidance stays in the existing maintenance and diagnostic surfaces. Identity,
permissions, collaboration context, and reply delivery checks remain in place.
