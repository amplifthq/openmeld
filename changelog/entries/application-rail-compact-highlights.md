title: Refine the application and Spaces sidebars
kind: improved
areas: ["navigation"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

The compact sidebar now highlights each navigation icon without covering its
label. Attention markers sit beside the icons, and organization and account
avatars fill matching shape-aware squares. The Spaces sidebar now keeps creation
and view controls compact, with view and filters grouped in one menu and a
shorter search field.
On phones, Inbox and Spaces status markers follow their labels, and navigation
utilities and the account menu sit in a vertical footer at the bottom of the drawer.
Team now places its people filters beside the Add menu and uses the same compact
search field style. Its directory filters use clear icons for groups and
computer states.
The Spaces and Team sidebars now put the collapse button beside their titles,
and show the expand button in the content header when collapsed. The button also
remains available when a collapsed sidebar is revealed from the edge. The Spaces
directory shows a single refresh warning when its saved conversation list cannot
be updated.
The Agents directory now uses the same quiet search, filter, and collapse controls
as People, showing their background on hover instead of at rest. Its bulk
selection button uses the same compact icon style and reveals its label on hover.
