title: More useful diagnostics when a Space takes too long to open
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "web"]
audiences: ["users", "operators"]
---

If a Space remains on the access-check screen, you can copy a diagnostic ID
to include with your report. Opening diagnostics now distinguish access checks,
conversation rendering, and composer availability, with request correlation
and cache observations to help locate delays without recording message content.
