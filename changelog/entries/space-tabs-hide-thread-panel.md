id: space-tabs-hide-thread-panel
title: Show Tasks and Files when switching away from a Thread
kind: fixed
areas: ["spaces", "desktop", "web"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Switching to Tasks or Files now hides an open Thread panel so it no longer
covers the selected view. Returning to Messages restores the selected Thread
and its unsent reply. This also applies while the Thread is loading.
