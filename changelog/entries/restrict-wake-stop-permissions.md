---
id: restrict-wake-stop-permissions
title: Keep Wake stop controls with the requester and agent owner
kind: security
areas: ["spaces", "agents"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Only the person who started a Wake or the agent's owner can stop it, while they
have permission to participate in the Space. Other members can follow progress
without being able to interrupt the work. The same restriction applies to API
and CLI requests.
