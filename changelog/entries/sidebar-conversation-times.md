---
id: sidebar-conversation-times
title: Show conversation times consistently in the sidebar
kind: fixed
areas: ["web"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Spaces show the latest conversation time when no status indicator is present,
or their creation time when empty. Direct Messages also show a time when empty.
Chats with only your messages now say “No replies yet” instead of “No messages
yet.” Times remain visible in narrower sidebars.
