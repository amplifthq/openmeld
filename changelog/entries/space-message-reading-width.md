id: space-message-reading-width
title: Give Space messages more reading room
kind: fixed
areas: ["spaces", "messages"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

In the Full width conversation layout, long Space and direct messages now use the available width instead of stopping at a fixed limit, while short messages stay fitted to their content. Floating message actions move over the message edge when needed instead of leaving unused space beside the text. When a long message scrolls past the top, its actions align with the message's lower edge: beside the bubble when there is room, or across its edge when there is not.
