title: Keep your place in Spaces and find unread replies
kind: improved
areas: ["web", "spaces", "inbox"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Selecting Spaces again in an open desktop conversation keeps your Thread and
reading position. The conversation list shows where unread messages are, even
when an Agent is working. Unread shortcuts identify where to jump, and the Space
menu offers **Mark all as read**. Personal replies and requests still draw your
attention in Inbox, while routine conversation updates stay available to browse.
