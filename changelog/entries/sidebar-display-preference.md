In OpenMeld Web, right-click the main sidebar to choose Icons and text or
Icons only. The same choices appear as preview cards in
Settings → Appearance → Sidebar. Your
choice is saved in this browser and stays in sync across tabs. Icons only makes
the sidebar narrower to give your work more room.
