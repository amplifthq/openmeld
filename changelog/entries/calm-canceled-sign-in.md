id: calm-canceled-sign-in
title: Canceled sign-in returns to the sign-in page
kind: fixed
areas: ["authentication", "web"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Canceling sign-in brings you back to the sign-in page, including when you
started from sign-up or a magic link. OpenMeld no longer shows the raw
authentication error page when a sign-in is abandoned. A different sign-in
failure still shows a message on that page.
