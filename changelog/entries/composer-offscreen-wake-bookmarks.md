id: composer-offscreen-wake-bookmarks
title: Keep track of agent work while reading a conversation
kind: new
areas: ["spaces", "composer"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

When you scroll away from work you started, a compact status beside Send lets
you return to it. Multiple active Wakes appear in a short list. The shortcut
disappears when the working status is back in view, keeping the conversation
and Composer from showing the same information twice.
