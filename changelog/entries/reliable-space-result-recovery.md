id: reliable-space-result-recovery
title: Recover saved Space results reliably
kind: fixed
areas: ["spaces", "wake"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Opening a Space no longer shows an error or a disabled recovery button when
OpenMeld cannot check an old Wake's recovery options. OpenMeld now finds saved
late results through the correct Space result record, so **Recover result**
appears when a real retained result is available.
