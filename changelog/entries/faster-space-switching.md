title: Faster switching between Spaces
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "desktop", "web"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Previously opened Spaces reuse their saved conversations more quickly. Switching
back to Messages also keeps an unfinished draft and reading position, while new
messages continue to update in the background. When a new Inbox message arrives,
the Space sidebar refreshes its preview too.
