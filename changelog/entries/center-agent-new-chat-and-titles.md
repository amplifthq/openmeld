kind: improved
areas: ["center-agent", "web"]

Open a Center Agent conversation's title to search chat history, switch chats,
or choose **New chat** to start a separate private conversation. New chats receive short titles that summarize your request
in its language, generated in the background without delaying the conversation.
Names you choose yourself always take priority.

The sidebar resize highlight now extends evenly toward both edges.
