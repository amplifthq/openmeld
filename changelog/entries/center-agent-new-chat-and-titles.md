title: Start fresh Center Agent chats with concise titles
kind: improved
areas: ["center-agent", "web"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Open a Center Agent conversation's title to search chat history, switch chats,
or choose **New chat** to start a separate private conversation. New chats receive short titles that summarize your request
in its language, generated in the background without delaying the conversation.
Names you choose yourself always take priority.

The sidebar resize highlight now extends evenly toward both edges.

Direct messages keep avatars beside the message bubbles and leave out repeated
names and Agent attribution. Your avatar follows your left or right message
preference, and message actions keep their own row below each bubble.

Group conversations also place avatars beside messages, with smaller, muted
names above the bubbles. Spacing between messages is tighter in both group
conversations and direct messages, while actions retain their own row.

Sidebar message previews are easier to distinguish from conversation titles,
and empty conversations no longer show placeholder preview text.

Thread panels can be resized wider on large screens without squeezing the main
conversation into an unreadable column. In smaller windows, Thread opens across
the conversation area and closing it returns to the main conversation.

From a Thread's original message details, choose **Show message in Messages**
to locate and highlight it in the main conversation while keeping the Thread
open, including when the original message is outside the loaded history.

Archive conversation is now in the direct message header's menu, with a
confirmation before the conversation is hidden from your list.
