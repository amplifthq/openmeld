Open a Center Agent chat's title to find earlier chats, switch conversations,
or start a new private chat. New chats receive short titles based on your
request without delaying the reply. A title you choose stays in place. You
can also archive a direct message from its header after confirming.

Conversation messages and sidebar previews are easier to scan. On wider
screens, resize a Thread while keeping the main conversation readable. From
message details, jump back to the message in the main conversation while the
Thread stays open.
