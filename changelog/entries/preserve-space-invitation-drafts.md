id: preserve-space-invitation-drafts
title: Keep the invitation you are writing
kind: fixed
areas: ["spaces", "invitations"]
audiences: ["users"]
evidence: ["pr:1156"]
---

Switching between Share link and Invite people keeps your unsent invitations,
with separate drafts for guests and teammates. Sending or resending another
invitation no longer clears newer input. Failed permission checks show a retry
action instead of leaving the invitation panel blank.
