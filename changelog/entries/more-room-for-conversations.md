id: more-room-for-conversations
title: Make more room for conversations
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "web", "settings"]
audiences: ["members"]
---

Pinned messages and files share one preview that you can dismiss and restore
from the Space header. Hide individual bottom toolbar shortcuts and restore
them in Appearance settings; hiding every shortcut gives the conversation more
room. Computer remains in the account menu, with attention indicators on the
menu item and your avatar. Display preferences are remembered in this browser.

Space tabs move beside the title while you read earlier messages, leaving more
room for the conversation. The header expands again at the latest messages.
Its position stays the same when you switch to Files, and this browser remembers
each Space's position when you leave and return.
Scrolling away from the latest messages avoids a full conversation redraw,
keeping the header transition responsive in long conversations. Toolbar
shortcuts in Appearance settings sit side by side and wrap on smaller screens.
