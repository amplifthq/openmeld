OpenMeld's public website has its own build and release at openmeld.ai, while
the app opens at app.openmeld.ai. Website updates can ship independently of the
app. Existing workspace links take you to the same place, and installer and
download URLs stay available.

Older plugin connection replies remain usable after the move, and installed
web app shortcuts explain how to open the new app. Account email links preserve
the intent to add another account, and password reset now sends a working email.
