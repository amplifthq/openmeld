areas: ["web"]

The application rail now stacks organization switching, Search, Inbox, and
Create as circular controls. Select the plus button to open New Agent and
New Space beside it. Organization avatars fill their circular button, while
page destinations keep their familiar rectangular shape.

The conversation sidebar still collapses when dragged closed. It stays hidden
when the pointer approaches the edge; use the sidebar button in the conversation
header to reopen it.
