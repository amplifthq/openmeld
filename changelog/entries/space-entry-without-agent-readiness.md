title: Open conversations without waiting for Agent readiness
kind: improved
areas: ["spaces", "web", "desktop"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Opening a Space no longer waits for Agent readiness or computer health just to
confirm access. Hovering or focusing a conversation prepares that check for your
next click. Cold conversations show their familiar loading layout and known title
while access is checked, with messages and sending kept behind the access gate.
