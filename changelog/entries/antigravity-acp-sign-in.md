title: Sign in to Antigravity ACP and verify its status
kind: improved
areas: ["agents", "cli"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Sign in to the selected official Antigravity ACP installation with
`openmeld agents login antigravity`. OpenMeld verifies that a new session can
use the saved sign-in. Account checks and agent runs do not open login windows.

Computer details show a sign-in command when it is needed. The legacy CLI shows
Installed when its account cannot be checked. Subscription usage remains
unavailable.
