title: A quieter sidebar footer
kind: improved
areas: ["web"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Settings and the theme switcher slide in from the right when you hover over the
sidebar footer and slide out when you leave. The account menu fills the available
width when the actions are hidden, keeping its chevron at the right edge. Keyboard focus reveals them
immediately, touch devices keep them visible, and reduced motion removes the slide.
