Spaces and Direct messages are available together in the conversation sidebar.
Use Grouped to browse by section or switch to Recent to continue conversations
by their latest message. Both views keep the same pinned conversations and
preserve the conversation and draft you have open. Your view choice is
remembered in this browser for each workspace.

Sidebar search and the command center use the same conversation names and open
the matching Space, direct message, or Center Agent session.
