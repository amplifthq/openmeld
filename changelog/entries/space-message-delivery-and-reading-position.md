title: Keep Space conversations current when messages arrive
kind: fixed
areas: ["spaces", "web"]

Space conversations now catch up on messages missed by the live connection, even when newer activity arrives first. An Inbox update can prompt the open conversation to refresh sooner, and a new message clears the extra space left by jumping to a message near the end.
