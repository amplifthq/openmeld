id: computer-control-timeout-diagnostics
title: Trace Computer control timeouts across the Hub and OpenMeld Service
kind: improved
areas: ["computers", "core", "openmeld-service"]
audiences: ["operators"]
---

Operators can follow a power, installation, or catalog request from its Hub
trace into the Computer's Service log using the control request ID. The stages
show when the Service received the request, where local work waited, and
whether a response reached the Hub before its deadline.
