---
id: composer-human-mentions
title: Mention people without turning them into Agent work
kind: improved
areas: ["composer", "spaces", "inbox", "cli"]
audiences: ["members", "developers"]
---

Space composers now treat `@` for people as a Human Mention while keeping Wake
and Reference specific to Agents. Context-aware placeholders and a dedicated
`@` button make the same addressing flow easier to find in Spaces, Direct
Messages, and Threads. Agent names keep their Wake or Reference choice inline,
while people appear as fixed Mention tokens. Agent Direct Messages stay focused
on writing and sending instead of repeating an optional `@` control and hint.
Mentions remain attached to the message after send, refresh, and live delivery,
while older clients keep their compatible Space message shape.
