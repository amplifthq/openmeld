The Spaces page now follows your sidebar groups and saved sorting, with
conversation previews, activity times, member avatars, and status filters.
Search opens when you need it, and New Space stays in the page header.

Discover separates previewing a public Space from joining it. Previews use the
same conversation and access checks as opening the Space.
