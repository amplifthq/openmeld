title: Preview replies in Messages
kind: improved
areas: ["web"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Messages shows the other person's or Agent's latest reply beneath their name,
with its time on the right. Today's replies show a time, yesterday's show
Yesterday, and older replies show how many days ago they arrived. Conversations
without a reply show No messages yet. The compact layout also applies to pinned
conversations and personal groups.
