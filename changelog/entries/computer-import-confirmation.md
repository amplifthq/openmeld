---
id: computer-import-confirmation
title: Save Computer results without rechecking restored files
kind: improved
areas: ["computer", "center-agent"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

After a Computer restart, files that have been restored and safely confirmed
do not need another full directory check before new results can be saved.
New changes still require confirmation, and interrupted transfers resume from
their saved progress. File permissions and modification times are preserved
when content stays the same.
