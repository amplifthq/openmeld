kind: improved
areas: ["web", "desktop", "spaces", "inbox", "team"]
audiences: ["users"]

Right-click conversations, messages, Inbox items, and Team resources to open
their common actions. Pin conversations, copy links, mark Inbox items as read,
star people, and reach existing Agent, Skill, and file actions from the item
you are working with. Menus use the same actions and permissions as their
existing controls, with consistent pointer and keyboard behavior.
