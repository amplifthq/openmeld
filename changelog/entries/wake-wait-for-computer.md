title: Let a Wake wait for its computer
kind: new
areas: ["spaces", "agents"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

You can choose to let a Space Wake wait for its selected computer to come online,
with a start deadline and a cancellation action. OpenMeld checks access and the
Agent's execution settings again before starting. Expired, cancelled, and failed
Wakes do not restart when a computer reconnects, and interrupted work continues
to use evidence-based result recovery.
