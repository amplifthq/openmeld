id: wake-wait-for-computer
title: Let a Wake wait for its computer
kind: new
areas: ["spaces", "agents"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

When an Agent's computer is offline, choose **Send and wait** to leave a task for
up to 24 hours. OpenMeld uses the Agent's connected computer automatically, shows
the waiting state, and lets you cancel. Message details show the exact expiry.
After expiry, **Send again** prepares a new request for you to review and send.
OpenMeld checks access and execution settings again before starting. Expired,
cancelled, and failed Wakes do not restart when a computer reconnects, and
interrupted work continues to use evidence-based result recovery.
