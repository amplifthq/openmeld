Spaces navigation now opens the Space directory, with Direct messages beside
Your Spaces. The conversation sidebar defaults to Recent, remembers a manually
chosen view, and includes clearer menu icons without a duplicate heading dot.

Desk starts with Welcome Space for both new and existing users, reusing one when
available and creating one when needed. Other Spaces are added by the user.
