id: agent-conversation-identity
title: Clearer Agent identity and replies
kind: fixed
areas: ["spaces", "chats", "agents"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Agent replies replace their working status without leaving a duplicate row in
Spaces and chats. Teammates' Agents show consistent avatars in the sidebar and
conversation. Local Agent messages identify the owner, and profile popovers
show the owner's name and avatar with availability beside the Agent name.
Chat headers show viewing access beside the title, and read-only composers
explain how to join without repeating the same instruction.
Members panels group Agents under their owners, including owners who have not
joined the conversation.
