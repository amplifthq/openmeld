title: Identify and retry the part of the conversation sidebar that failed
kind: fixed
areas: ["web", "desktop"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Conversation sidebar errors identify whether conversations, member information,
Center Agent conversations, or sidebar settings failed to load. Refresh failures
keep saved content visible and explain that it may be out of date. Each Retry
button retries only the affected request.
