title: Start team collaboration from your Personal Workspace
kind: improved
areas: ["onboarding", "organizations", "spaces"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Invitation entry points in your Personal Workspace now explain how to create
or choose an organization. After you create or select one, OpenMeld opens its
invitation settings so you can continue. Your Personal Spaces, agents, and files
stay in your Personal Workspace. Channels and organization settings use the
same guidance, and the workspace choice shows its full description.
