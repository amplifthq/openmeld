The application rail keeps Center Agent, Messenger, and Team within reach while
the middle column shows the relevant conversation or member list. Collapse or
resize that list to give your current work more room without losing your message
draft. Browse spaces is a compass in the Messenger header in both sidebar views,
and the plus menu offers New message and New Space. When a conversation
search has no results, Find someone carries your search into the organization
picker so you can message a teammate you have not spoken with before.
