id: agent-directory-management
title: Find and manage Agents more easily
kind: improved
areas: ["agents", "web"]
audiences: ["users"]
---

Filter Agents by Local or Remote and by current availability, search within
each person's list by name, model, or vendor, and remove individual filter
chips or reset the view. Icons make filter, sort, and grouping choices easier
to recognize.
Collapse a person's Agent list to make room for other people without losing
your search or filters.

Select your Agents and delete several at once, with an exact confirmation. The
list clears immediately while deletion runs, then shows a summary and restores
only Agents whose deletion could not be confirmed. Creation history is beside
your name and includes search, filters, and a Needs attention shortcut.
